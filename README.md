
## The Acronix Manifesto

The Acronix Manifesto describes what we (Acronix Labs), believe in and the priniciples we stand for. Publicly published under the `Creative Commons Attribution Share-Alike 4.0` 

Pull requests will be ignored. 
This document is not a warranty or a garuntee, it is simply what we as an organization stand for.

## Manifesto

# Agents
AI is here to help, not hinder the process, and by collaborating with agents, we as builders will create more powerful creations. 


# Telemetry

Acronix is against corporate spyware, and will never allow telemetry to take place in it's projects that the user is not aware about: opt in - off by default, always

# Licensing

Acronix chooses to keep their products under different licenses, not just `MIT` or `GPL`. We may even create Fair Code or commercial projects.

# Subscriptions

Acronix prefers larger One Time Purchases to reccuring subscriptions. 
**cloud services** being harder to provide, we may include subscriptions for those.
